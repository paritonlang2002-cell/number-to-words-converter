🧠 How the logic works

  • Split the number into groups of three digits.
  • Convert each group into words using units, tens, and hundreds.
  • Attach the appropriate scale, such as thousand, million, lakh, or crore.
  • Handle zero, negative values, decimals, and invalid input separately.
